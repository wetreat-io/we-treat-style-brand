This is the We Treat brand guidelines page, intended for both human and AI consumption. To use this file, simply feed this URL to your AI of choice, or copy the source code and paste it into your project, OR copy the CSS from the View/Copy CSS button in the bottom left and paste into your AI of choice.

This is meant to be used as an additive to any basic layout and styling system (bootstrap, tailwind, custom, etc.), or as a standalone custom system (or raw inline css) for producing brand consistent collateral (websites, print, promotional graphics, images, etc.).

We use a very limited palette and focus on clean design with subtle gradients and rounded corners.

The We Treat brand voice is 75% Sage (to seek and share truth, wisdom, and understanding) and 25% Ruler (create order, stability, leadership, and control).
